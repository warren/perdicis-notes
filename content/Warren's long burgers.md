# Warren's long burgers
#publish 
Opened [[2022-11-08]]. From [[Musings on everything else]].

I love upscale burgers. I even maintain a burger [[The best burger in the bay area|tier list]] on this blog! And a common issue I've noticed at many upscale restaurants is that they make their burgers way too tall:

![[PXL_20230429_021816691.MP.jpg]]
_Taller than evolution intended ([[Son and Garden]])_

To eat something like this, your options are either: (A) chomp partial bites into the burger, never quite slicing top-to-bottom thereby missing its full range of flavors; or (B) dislocate your jaw to eat it in one bite like a snake, or something like that.

There _is_ a better way. So I'm writing this open letter to all restaurants; there exists a third option (C): **Just use wider buns, and spread your burger ingredients flatter.**

I'm calling this idea Warren's long burgers. Spread the word!
