# Yotta Kosho
#publish 
_Part of a series on [[Cocktails]]._

A replica cocktail from [Roka Akor](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aUPE7AXTxPjwMrje7), a restaurant in San Francisco. I first tried it [[2025-W21|2025-05-24]]. The bartender didn't know the exact proportions but knew the preparation steps.

![[Pasted image 20250526123208.png]]

## Flavor profile
Smooth, herbal, not-too-sweet.

## Ingredients
Habenero vodka  
House sake (junmai style - least sweet)  
Shochu (distilled soju. just a little bit)

Shiso leaf (don't put in water, Keep in fridge)  
Cucumbers 
  
^ total 3oz after straining  
  
Lime  
Simple  
Elderflower liqueur  

## Preparation
Top 3 alcohols in the ingredient list --> add a bunch of shiso and cucumber, put it in a Ziploc so it won't dissolve in hot water, cook for 10-15min

Cool it down and strain, add 1/2oz lime, 1/4oz elderflower liqueur, splash of simple.

Done!

For the top 3 alcohols, the proportions are roughly:
1 part (bottle) vodka
1/2 part sake
1/2 part soju
