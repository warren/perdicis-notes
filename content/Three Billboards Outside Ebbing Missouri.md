# Three Billboards Outside Ebbing Missouri
#publish 
Opened [[2026-10-02]]. Related to [[Movies I've seen]].


- The most Cohen Bros film I've seen that isn't actually a Cohen Bros film
    - The gut punch in the last ~15 minutes in particular made me feel that way.
- Themes of forgiveness and vengeance appear in unexpected places.
    - I enjoyed how all the characters can be vaguely understood through their alignment on this spectrum. None of them -- save Mildred -- are _completely_ committed to forgiveness nor vengeance, making Mildred an interesting character to follow as we observe how other characters evolve due to her behavior.
- In general, I enjoyed how much restraint the film exercised with its character writing.
    - There aren't any one-liners.
    - Learning the characters' behaviors allows you to sort of predict how they'll react to something, which affords a looot of refreshing nonverbal communication. A small wincing expression here, a stiff upper lip there, ... so much is communicated without getting in your face. I found that great.
