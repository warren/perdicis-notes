## Flavor profile
Straightforward, smooth, and not too sweet.

## Ingredients
- 1oz mezcal or gin
- 1oz lime juice
- 1oz green chartreuse
- 1oz simple syrup infused with sage

Idea for a garnish: fried sage. It would almost be like a chip.
