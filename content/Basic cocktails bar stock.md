# Basic cocktails bar stock
#publish 
Opened [[2025-01-13]]. Sharded out of [[Cocktails]], circa [[2024-03-01]].

This is the bar stock necessary to make the following simple cocktails:
- [[Martini]]: Gin, dry vermouth, bitters
- [[Negroni]]: Gin, sweet vermouth, campari
- [[Manhattan]]: Whiskey, dry vermouth, bitters
- [[Margarita]]: Tequila, triple sec, lime juice, syrup
- [[Daquiri]]: Rum, lime juice, syrup
- Gin and tonic: Gin, tonic water
- Rum and coke: Rum, coke
- Screwdriver: Orange juice, vodka

I wrote this content when I was first starting out with a home bar so it's a little rough around the edges.

## Bar stock
Alcohol
- Gin
- Dry vermouth
- Sweet vermouth
- Whiskey
- Tequila
- Triple sec
- Rum
- Vodka (not in any of these drinks, but you need it)
    - Skimp on the price. Kavlana might be good.

Misc
- Orange bitters
- Angostura bitters
- Lime juice
- Agave syrup (just for the margarita)
- Simple syrup

Mixers
- Tonic water
- Coke
- Orange juice
    - Cranberry juice is better for vodka shots imo

Accessories
- Solo cups (for parties)
- Coupe glasses
    - Everyone agrees martini glasses suck. They're too big and spill. Coupe glasses are better. Get those.
        - Furthermore, [[Fun facts|fun fact]]: Cocktail and martini glasses are the same as coupe glasses, just adapted for different drinking habits.
