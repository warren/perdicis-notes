## Flavor profile
Sweet, smoky, and tangs of holidaytime flavors from a tea blend (cinnamon, hibiscus, chamomile, chicory, apple and orange). Maybe it will taste like a cinnamon roll.

## Ingredients
- 4oz TJ's spiced cider
- 1oz water brewed celestial apple tea (basically 1/4 of a teabag)
- 2oz bourbon
- 1oz lemon juice
- 1/4tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4tsp ground ginger
- garnish with any of the following:
    - 1 sprig rosemary
    - 1 apple slice
    - 1 cinnamon stick
