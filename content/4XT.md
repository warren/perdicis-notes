## Flavor profile
Sweet, apple cider-y, and hints of holidaytime flavors from a tea blend (cinnamon, hibiscus, chamomile, chicory, apple and orange).

## Ingredients
- 4oz TJ's spiced cider
- 2oz bourbon
- 1/4tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4tsp ground ginger
- Garnish with any of the following:
    - 1 sprig rosemary
    - 1 apple slice
    - 1 cinnamon stick

\*Originally also used 1oz water brewed celestial apple tea (basically 1/4 of a teabag) in a serving, but when preparing this for [[May Day Cocktail Menu|May Day]] I will try omitting this part because I don't think a simpler flavor is better.

## Preparation
- Heat the cider first, but not to a boil.
- Add bourbon, spices to taste.
- Mix till the consistency is even.
- Serve out of the pot!
