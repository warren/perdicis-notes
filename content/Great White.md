# Great White
#publish 
_Part of a series on [[Cocktails]]._

A replica cocktail from [Mac's Shack](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QJXHYQPWGtUbQ18L8), a seafood restaurant in Wellfleet, Cape Cod. I first tried it [[2026-07-06]]. Sorta guessing at the proportions.

![[Pasted image 20260710133743.png]]

## Flavor profile
Gentle on the palette / goes down easy, creamy rather than sweet, very coconut-y

## Ingredients
- 1.5oz vodka
- 0.75oz coconut rum
- 0.75oz Coco Lopez
    - (this is the brand used for pina coladas too btw. Probably best to get it specifically)
- 0.75oz lime juice

## Preparation
- Add everything to a cocktail shaker with plenty of ice.
- Shake vigorously for >=15 seconds. Cream of coconut is thick and requires a hard shake with ice to emulsify properly with the liquids.
- Double strain into a chilled martini glass (to catch any fine ice shards or coconut solids)
- (Optional) Garnish with a lime
